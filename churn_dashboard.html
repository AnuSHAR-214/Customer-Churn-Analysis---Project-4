    What to do about it

    Four moves, ordered by how much churn they sit in front of.

    • Contract mixOffer a discount or loyalty credit to move month-to-month customers onto annual terms. This is the single largest lever on the board.
    • First 90 daysBuild an onboarding and engagement program for new customers, where churn is concentrated and recoverable.
    • Fiber economicsReview fiber pricing and reliability against competitor offers — this segment pays the most and leaves the most.
    • Early warningFlag high-charge, low-tenure customers for proactive outreach before they reach a renewal decision.
